**Measured result: closed-loop correction produced zero percentage-point
improvement in satisfaction rate over one-shot configuration in every
scenario of the systematic sweep.** A naive pool across all scenarios
(ONE_SHOT 70%, CLOSED_LOOP 82%, +12pp) is a sampling artifact of unequal
per-scenario repetition counts, not a measured effect, and is not reported
as a finding (see `docs/final_report.md`, Results and Failure analysis).
